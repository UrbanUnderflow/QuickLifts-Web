BOARD CONSENT — PULSE INTELLIGENCE LABS, INC.

Reciprocal Strategic Equity Authorization

UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

OF PULSE INTELLIGENCE LABS, INC.

The undersigned, Tremaine Grant, constituting the sole director of Pulse Intelligence Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), hereby adopts the following resolutions. This consent is dated September 23, 2026 and becomes effective upon signature and delivery by every member of the Board then serving, subject to the Company’s governing documents and applicable law.

WHEREAS

The Company is entering into a Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement with EDNA, Inc. (“EDNA”) relating to the Parties’ strategic commercial relationship, integrated services, product and technical integration, pilot collaboration, customer development, and long-term operating relationship;

WHEREAS, the Company and EDNA intend to enter into a Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter under which each company grants the other a 200,000-common-share Strategic Share Award without a cash purchase requirement and a separate Warrant to purchase up to 200,000 additional common shares, each vesting over four years with a one-year cliff, subject to the required approvals and closing conditions;

WHEREAS, the Board has determined that reciprocal company-to-company strategic equity participation may align the long-term interests of the Company and EDNA and support the development of the strategic relationship contemplated by the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement;

WHEREAS, the Board has reviewed or has been presented with the material terms of the proposed Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter and the proposed strategic partnership warrant or other equity instrument to be issued by the Company;

WHEREAS, the Board intends that the Company’s issuance to EDNA be undertaken on a reciprocal basis, with EDNA authorizing and issuing substantially equivalent strategic equity rights to the Company, subject to differences reasonably required by the respective capitalization structures, corporate laws, governing documents, and classes of securities of the two companies;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT:

RESOLVED

1. Approval of Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter

The Company is hereby authorized to enter into the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter with EDNA, substantially in the form presented to the Board, with such non-material modifications as the appropriate officers of the Company and Company counsel determine to be necessary or advisable.

2. Reciprocal Nature of Authorization

The strategic equity authorization contemplated by these resolutions is expressly intended to be reciprocal.

The effectiveness of both the Company’s Share Award and its purchase Warrant in favor of EDNA is conditioned upon EDNA:

obtaining its own required board, stockholder, investor, and other corporate approvals;

executing the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter;

authorizing a reciprocal 200,000-common-share Strategic Share Award and separate purchase Warrant for up to 200,000 additional common shares in favor of the Company, with matching vesting and substantive protections; and

executing definitive equity documentation reasonably consistent with the economic and legal principles applicable to the Company’s issuance.

Procedural differences required by applicable law or governing documents may be used, but both reciprocal components shall retain their fixed common-share counts, matching vesting schedules, and substantive protections. Any change of security class or fixed share count requires both Parties’ written agreement and all required corporate approvals, except for expressly permitted proportionate corporate adjustments. Equal share counts do not represent a promise of equal ownership percentages or equal dollar values.

3. Authorization of Company Share Award and Purchase Warrant

Subject to the conditions in these resolutions, the Company is authorized to grant to EDNA, Inc. (i) the 200,000-common-share Strategic Share Award under the Side Letter and (ii) a separate Warrant to purchase up to 200,000 additional common shares. The Share Award issues shares as vested without a cash purchase requirement. The Warrant issues shares only upon exercise of vested rights and satisfaction of its exercise terms. The combined maximum is 400,000 common shares, subject only to express proportionate corporate adjustments.

The Board approves exactly 200,000 common shares for the Share Award and a separate maximum of 200,000 common shares for the Warrant, subject only to proportionate stock-split and similar adjustments. Officers may not substitute a percentage-based grant or combine the two components into a single purchase right.

Both fixed share counts shall be stated expressly in the Side Letter and applicable Warrant. The Board shall approve the Warrant exercise price before it becomes effective; no historical advisor price is adopted by these resolutions.

The issuance shall remain subject to:

determination of the applicable exercise price;

availability of sufficient authorized shares;

securities-law compliance;

applicable board, stockholder, or investor approvals;

compliance with existing financing and investor agreements; and

execution of definitive documentation.

3.1 Strategic Share Award Issuance

The PIL Strategic Vesting Share Agreement dated September 23, 2026 constitutes the definitive PIL Strategic Share Award agreement and is approved together with the revised Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter and separate PIL Strategic Partnership Warrant, each dated September 23, 2026, in the forms attached to this consent. No unvested Share Award shares shall be issued at closing. The Company is authorized and directed to issue each vested installment to EDNA, Inc. on its applicable vesting date, including any expressly accelerated vesting date, and promptly record the issuance in the stock ledger. No exercise notice or cash purchase payment is required for those shares. Administrative delay shall not forfeit an accrued right to issuance. Actual common-stock rights attach to issued shares, subject to lawful restrictions; no stockholder rights attach solely to unissued award rights.

3.2 Consideration and Separate Warrant Mechanics

The Board authorizes the recipient’s strategic undertakings and contributions under the Partnership Agreement and Side Letter as non-cash consideration for the Share Award, subject to a documented determination of legally sufficient value, including applicable par-value requirements, before the award becomes effective. The share award does not discharge otherwise payable commercial obligations. Separately, vested Warrant rights may be exercised for the approved exercise price, subject to express cashless-exercise provisions. The Warrant’s ten-year term runs from its Issue Date and does not apply to the Share Award.

3.3 Share Reservation

The proposed capitalization setup provides a separate 1,600,000-share Equity Incentive Plan reserve and 400,000-share strategic reserve, divided into 200,000 Share Award shares and 200,000 Warrant shares. This consent conditionally authorizes the strategic reservation only upon confirmation of sufficient authorized and uncommitted shares. It does not reduce the incentive reserve to 600,000 shares, amend the charter, approve an uncompleted founder share return, or substitute for separately required Plan and stockholder approvals. The officers must document the effective Plan approvals, any required founder share return, and reconciled stock ledger before closing. Existing awards must not be impaired or double counted.

3.4 Repurchase Option and Consideration Record

The Board expressly approves Section 3.5 of the attached PIL Strategic Vesting Share Agreement, including the option to repurchase issued Share Award shares, including vested shares, at a negotiated contractual price of $0.10 per share repurchased, or $20,000 for all 200,000 shares, after the specified fraud, intellectual-property theft, misconduct, or uncured material breach, subject to its notice, cure, final-determination, exercise and legal-limit provisions. Partial repurchases are proportional, and stock-split and similar adjustments shall preserve that full-award aggregate price. Adoption of this contractual price is subject to authorized bilateral execution of the matching reciprocal instruments and all closing conditions; the same price shall apply to EDNA’s repurchase of its reciprocal Share Award issued to PIL. This Board approval alone does not establish EDNA’s agreement. The option applies to Share Award shares; this consent does not extend it to Warrant shares. Before either instrument becomes effective, the Board must approve a written consideration schedule documenting the strategic noncash consideration and its legally sufficient aggregate value and per-share allocation separately from the contractual repurchase price. The Board must separately approve the Warrant exercise price and aggregate exercise cost. The $0.10 contractual repurchase price is not a determination of fair market value, noncash consideration value or tax basis, and this consent supplies no new amount for those values or for the separate Warrant exercise price.

3.5 Reciprocal Closing

PIL’s Share Award and Warrant shall have no force or effect until EDNA delivers its valid reciprocal Share Award and separate Warrant, required approvals, and capitalization certificate at coordinated closing. EDNA’s reciprocal Share Award must represent 2.0% of EDNA’s fully diluted capitalization at that closing. If the fixed 200,000 shares do not meet that condition, both Parties must sign an amendment and obtain all required corporate approvals before closing; there is no automatic change to either share count. The condition is an initial closing test, not a continuing anti-dilution right. The PIL Share Award’s Section 6 governs reciprocal delivery and later breach remedies.

4. Company-to-Company Strategic Ownership

The Board confirms that the equity contemplated by these resolutions constitutes a company-to-company strategic equity arrangement.

No equity or economic interest is authorized by these resolutions personally for any founder, officer, director, employee, advisor, contractor, stockholder, or other individual associated with EDNA.

The recipient of the Company’s strategic equity right shall be EDNA itself or another entity expressly approved by the Board and consistent with the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter.

5. Purpose of Issuance

The Board determines that the strategic equity issuance is being authorized in connection with the Parties’ anticipated strategic commercial relationship, which may include:

Joint Services commercialization;

product and technical integration;

pilot collaboration;

Clinical Handoff infrastructure;

customer and institutional development;

implementation collaboration;

strategic market development; and

long-term enterprise value associated with the relationship.

The strategic equity shall remain separate from customer revenue-sharing obligations, sales commissions, referral compensation, Grant funding, and other individual transaction-based compensation arrangements unless separately approved.

6. Securities-Law and Existing Investor Rights

The Board acknowledges that the Company and EDNA intend the reciprocal strategic equity rights to arise from the Parties’ commercial strategic relationship rather than from an investment of capital by either company.

However, neither these resolutions nor the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter shall conclusively determine the treatment of the issuance under applicable securities laws, existing financing documents, investor-rights agreements, preemptive rights, rights of first offer, most-favored-nation provisions, conversion provisions, or similar contractual rights.

The appropriate officers of the Company are directed to obtain advice from Company counsel and confirm all required approvals, consents, exemptions, notices, filings, and restrictions before issuance.

No issuance shall proceed in violation of a binding contractual right or applicable law.

7. Vesting

The Share Award and purchase Warrant shall each vest separately over forty-eight (48) months commencing September 11, 2026 as follows:

twenty-five percent (25%), equal to 50,000 shares under each component, shall vest on September 11, 2027; and

the remaining seventy-five percent (75%), equal to 150,000 shares under each component, shall vest monthly during months thirteen (13) through forty-eight (48), ending September 11, 2030, using the whole-share rounding formula in the Side Letter and Warrant.

Continued vesting shall be conditioned upon:

the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement remaining in effect;

EDNA not being in an uncured material breach of that agreement or the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter;

EDNA continuing to materially participate in the strategic relationship in good faith; and

EDNA not having committed an Uncurable Strategic Breach as defined in the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter.

The corresponding EDNA strategic equity instrument issued to the Company shall provide for substantially equivalent vesting conditions.

8. Effect of Termination

If the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement expires, is not renewed, or is otherwise terminated without an uncured material breach by EDNA:

equity properly vested through the effective termination date shall remain vested, subject to the definitive instrument; and

all unvested equity shall terminate as of the effective termination date.

If termination results from EDNA’s uncured material breach, unvested equity shall terminate in accordance with the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter and definitive warrant.

The Company shall receive substantially reciprocal treatment under the equity instrument issued by EDNA.

9. Uncurable Strategic Breach

If EDNA commits an Uncurable Strategic Breach as defined in the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter:

all then-unvested strategic equity granted by the Company shall terminate; and

treatment of vested securities shall be governed by the definitive instrument and applicable law.

The reciprocal equity instrument issued by EDNA to the Company shall provide substantially equivalent consequences if the Company commits a corresponding Uncurable Strategic Breach.

10. Change of Control

The Share Award and Warrant shall each provide substantially the following Change-of-Control treatment, applied separately to each component:

Upon a Change of Control of the Company occurring while the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement remains in effect and EDNA is not in an uncured material breach:

fifty percent (50%) of the then-unvested portion of the strategic equity shall accelerate and vest immediately prior to consummation of the Change of Control.

The remaining unvested portion shall accelerate if, in connection with or within twelve (12) months following the Change of Control:

the acquiring entity terminates the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement other than because of EDNA’s material breach;

the acquiring entity materially eliminates the strategic relationship;

the acquiring entity fails or refuses to assume or provide economically equivalent treatment for the strategic equity instrument; or

the acquiring entity is a material competitor of EDNA and EDNA exercises an applicable Change-of-Control termination or exclusivity right under Section 14.4A of the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement.

No acceleration shall apply in favor of EDNA if EDNA is then in an uncured material breach or has committed an Uncurable Strategic Breach.

The strategic equity instrument issued by EDNA to the Company shall provide substantially equivalent Change-of-Control treatment.

11. Financing Participation Right

The Company is authorized to grant EDNA the financing participation right described in the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter.

Subject to the definitive terms of such right, EDNA may elect to purchase securities in a Qualified Financing in an amount generally sufficient to maintain its then-current ownership percentage, subject to:

applicable securities law;

investor qualification requirements;

Board approval;

existing investor rights;

financing allocation limitations;

execution of applicable financing documents; and

the material terms of the applicable Qualified Financing.

The participation right is an opportunity to invest additional capital in a separately documented financing and does not guarantee any ownership percentage or increase either fixed 200,000-share component.

If EDNA declines or is unable to participate, its percentage ownership may be diluted.

EDNA shall provide the Company a substantially reciprocal financing participation right under its corresponding strategic equity documentation.

12. No Control or Governance Rights

Except for rights necessarily arising from the actual class of securities issued or separately approved in definitive documentation, the Share Award and purchase Warrant shall not provide EDNA with:

a board seat;

board observer rights;

management authority;

veto rights;

approval rights over ordinary-course business decisions;

control over Company financing;

control over PulseCheck;

control over Company Intellectual Property;

access to Company data;

fiduciary rights arising solely from the strategic relationship;

agency authority; or

authority to bind the Company.

The Company shall receive substantially equivalent treatment under the reciprocal equity instrument issued by EDNA.

13. No Intellectual Property or Data Rights

The Company’s issuance of strategic equity to EDNA shall not grant EDNA any ownership, access, inspection, license, or other rights in the Company’s:

Intellectual Property;

algorithms;

models;

model weights;

source code;

proprietary datasets;

customer information;

performance information;

data architecture;

Confidential Information; or

other proprietary systems,

except as expressly provided in the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement or another signed agreement.

The Company’s ownership of reciprocal equity issued by EDNA shall likewise not grant the Company any ownership, access, inspection, license, or other rights in EDNA’s clinical systems, clinical data, Intellectual Property, models, workflows, datasets, or Confidential Information except as separately agreed in writing.

14. No Cross-Company Capital Obligation

Neither the Company’s receipt of equity in EDNA nor EDNA’s receipt of equity in the Company shall obligate either Party to:

contribute additional capital;

guarantee debts;

fund operating losses;

participate in a future financing;

fund litigation;

guarantee customer obligations;

assume liabilities;

contribute Grant funding; or

provide other financial support to the other Party.

Any such obligation must be separately approved and documented in writing.

15. Transfer Restrictions

The Share Award, the purchase Warrant, and all shares issued under either component shall be subject to applicable securities laws, governing documents, investor agreements, transfer restrictions, rights of first refusal, restrictive legends, and their respective definitive terms.

Unless otherwise approved by the Board, neither unissued Share Award rights nor the unexercised Warrant may be transferred to:

a direct material competitor of the Company;

an entity acquiring EDNA primarily for purposes of obtaining the warrant; or

another third party where the transfer would reasonably be expected to create material competitive, regulatory, security, or governance risk to the Company.

The reciprocal instrument issued by EDNA shall provide substantially equivalent transfer protections for EDNA.

16. No Modification of Partnership Economics

The Board confirms that the reciprocal equity arrangement is separate from the commercial economics contained in the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement.

The Company’s issuance of strategic equity shall not:

reduce revenue otherwise payable to EDNA;

substitute for amounts payable under Joint Services contracts;

alter Shared Account economics;

modify Independent Contract rights;

modify the allocation of operational responsibility; or

create an ownership interest in EDNA’s commercial revenue.

The reciprocal equity issued by EDNA to the Company shall operate under the same principle.

17. Final Documentation

The appropriate officers of the Company are authorized to negotiate, finalize, execute, and deliver:

the Reciprocal Strategic Equity Side Letter;

the Strategic Share Award terms in the Side Letter and the separate purchase Warrant;

securities-law notices and filings;

capitalization updates;

stock ledger entries;

certificates or electronic equity records;

transfer-restriction documentation; and

any other documents reasonably necessary to implement these resolutions,

provided that such documentation remains materially consistent with these resolutions and the reciprocal economic arrangement approved herein.

18. Capitalization Confirmation

Before either component becomes effective, the officers shall verify and document sufficient authorized and uncommitted common shares for the combined 400,000-share reservation, the lawful source of those shares, required approvals, and the resulting ownership percentages. Capitalization disclosure does not recalculate either fixed 200,000-share component.

The calculation shall specify the securities included within Fully Diluted Capitalization, including the treatment, as applicable, of:

common stock;

preferred stock;

options;

warrants;

SAFEs;

convertible notes or other convertible instruments;

reserved option-pool shares; and

other rights to acquire Company equity.

The final calculation shall be approved in accordance with applicable corporate requirements.

19. Reciprocal Authorization Confirmation

Before either Company component becomes effective, an appropriate officer shall confirm satisfactory evidence that EDNA has authorized both reciprocal components, reserved sufficient common shares, approved its exercise price and consideration, and completed the coordinated closing conditions.

Neither Company component is required to become effective materially earlier than the corresponding EDNA components unless both Parties expressly agree otherwise in writing.

The Parties should use commercially reasonable efforts to execute and issue the reciprocal instruments contemporaneously.

20. Ratification

All lawful actions previously taken by any officer, director, or authorized representative of the Company in connection with the negotiation and preparation of the matters described in these resolutions are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved, provided that such ratification shall not authorize any action inconsistent with these resolutions, applicable law, or the Company’s governing documents.

21. Additional Closing and Governance Protections

RESOLVED, that before either component becomes effective, the officers shall obtain and retain the board-approved capitalization and share-reservation certificate required by Section 18.3 of the Side Letter, confirming the separate 200,000-share components, sufficient authorized and uncommitted shares, non-cash consideration for the Share Award, the approved Warrant price, and all required approvals. The certificate shall not adjust the fixed share counts based on a percentage calculation.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that any remedy based upon verified intentional circumvention of a Shared Account shall apply only to a specifically documented customer or counterparty, applicable organizational unit, and defined opportunity or scope, and shall not automatically extend to unrelated departments, affiliates, teams, or opportunities merely because they share a parent organization.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that unauthorized misconduct by a salesperson, fundraiser, consultant, advisor, or other representative shall not automatically constitute an Uncurable Strategic Breach by EDNA unless such conduct is legally attributable to EDNA, was authorized, directed, knowingly ratified, or materially enabled by EDNA, or EDNA fails to take commercially reasonable corrective action after notice.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the appropriate officers are authorized to include a cashless or net-exercise mechanism in connection with a Change of Control where permitted by applicable law and Company governing documents, so long as the reciprocal EDNA instrument provides substantially equivalent economic treatment.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Company’s strategic equity, financing participation rights, and relationship with EDNA, Inc. shall not give any investor, lender, creditor, special purpose vehicle, board designee, board observer, Affiliate, or other financing-related third party of EDNA, Inc. any authority to bind the Company, direct the Company’s financing or ordinary operations, obtain the Company’s protected data or Intellectual Property, or require the Company to accept financing, transfer assets, change capitalization, or waive rights.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the appropriate officers are authorized and directed to preserve the Company’s independent capital-formation authority. The Company is not required to raise equal capital, maintain an equivalent valuation, use the same SPV, accept the same investors, or match the financing terms of EDNA, Inc. The reciprocal financing participation right remains a limited opportunity to invest and shall not operate as a financing veto.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that if a direct material competitor of the Company obtains a board seat, board-observer right, enhanced information right, or comparable governance right in EDNA, Inc., the Company’s officers shall enforce the confidentiality, information-firewall, data, and Intellectual Property protections contained in the Partnership Agreement, Side Letter, and Warrants; provided that this resolution does not create a general Company approval right over EDNA, Inc.’s investors.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that no Financial Distress Event (as defined in the Strategic Partnership and Integration Agreement), creditor action, insolvency proceeding, or financing default affecting EDNA, Inc. shall create a cross-default by the Company, obligate the Company to fund or guarantee EDNA, Inc., or give any creditor, receiver, trustee, assignee, investor, lender, or successor of EDNA, Inc. greater rights in the Company than EDNA, Inc. possessed under the definitive agreements and applicable law.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that an Affiliate, subsidiary, successor, or SPV shall not be used by either Party to evade the reciprocal equity, Shared Account, data, Intellectual Property, non-circumvention, or financing-independence protections of the definitive agreements. Any permitted Affiliate transfer of an unissued award right or unexercised Warrant shall remain subject to the applicable competitor-transfer restrictions and continuing-affiliate requirements.

22. Protected Data and Joint Research Implementation

RESOLVED, that approval of the strategic equity arrangement does not authorize access to or use of the counterparty’s Protected Data or Joint Research Data and does not expand any operational, clinical, performance, research, publication, model-training, commercialization, or data rights; and

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers are authorized and directed to implement the four-lane framework in the Partnership Agreement and Exhibit A, including the Operational Performance Lane, Clinical Lane, Clinical Handoff Lane, and Joint Research Lane, on a reciprocal basis, with HIPAA and other applicable law providing additional protections where EDNA Protected Clinical Data constitutes PHI.

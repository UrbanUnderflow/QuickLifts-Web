01 / The operating process

Renewal starts
at kickoff.

Lead with a proposed definition of success. Refine it with the school, then keep evidence, actions and the purchasing timeline connected.

02 / The meeting rhythm

The right people.
The right frequency.

Coaches shape the day-to-day experience. The AD or budget owner joins when objectives, value and the next investment are being decided.

Every two weeks · 25–30 min

Working review

In the room: your pilot lead, the school coordinator and one coach representative. If the coach is the coordinator, keep it to that coach and your lead.

  • Review participation and feedback.
  • Understand friction and missing data.
  • Choose one or two adjustments.
  • Assign an owner and a due date.

Beforehand: refresh the Proof Package and collect Coach Check-Ins.

Pilot midpoint · 45 min

Value review

In the room: you, the AD or budget decision-maker, school champion and coach representative.

  • Assess the evidence against agreed targets.
  • Surface concerns that could block purchase.
  • Confirm possible paid scope and budget source.
  • Confirm procurement steps and decision date.

Include the relevant care lead for aggregate support-pathway discussion.

Before the budget deadline

Renewal decision

In the room: you, the AD, school champion and budget or purchasing owner.

  • Present a clear recommendation.
  • Review results and unresolved gaps.
  • Decide scope, price and responsibilities.
  • Record the decision and contracting steps.

Start planning 4–6 weeks before the purchasing deadline; allow longer when procurement requires it.

04 / The living Proof Package

Twelve measures.
A defensible story.

Update before each working review. Show the reporting window, denominator, response coverage and missing data alongside every result.

Agree the success rules before reviewing outcomes. Three check-ins weekly is an athlete benchmark; the school-level target is the share of eligible athletes or athlete-weeks meeting it. Agree that share explicitly. Counts and wellbeing trends stay descriptive. Overlapping participation measures do not count as three independent proofs of benefit.
Collection readiness: verify live data for all twelve measures before making commitments. Coach usefulness, continuation intent, scheduled Coach Check-Ins and confirmed care connections need their collection workflows confirmed or added. Starting Point completion and annual mental-health screening are separate records.
05 / Turn evidence into a recommendation

Choose the next
right commitment.

Lead the package with the recommended decision and its reasons. Include scope, pricing, staffing needs, limitations and a decision date.

Continue at paid scope

Evidence supports value at the current scale, with a willing buyer, funding route and workable delivery plan. Define the paid term and responsibilities.

Expand deliberately

Current teams demonstrate value and implementation capacity supports more teams. Explain why the proposed expansion size is feasible.

Run a bounded extension

A specific unanswered question blocks the decision. Name the evidence needed, owner, cost, end date and decision rule. Avoid open-ended extensions.

Close and learn

Value, fit, funding or delivery conditions do not support continuation. Record the reason, agreed closeout responsibilities and lessons for the next pilot.

No automatic pass score. Zero flagged incidents means no qualifying flags were recorded. No incidents needing care means connection to care is not applicable. Neither earns a success or failure point.

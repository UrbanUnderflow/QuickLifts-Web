03 · Safety and care

We found the athletes who weren't going to ask

Six athletes were flagged for follow-up through Nora check-ins or the season screenings. Each one was connected to a licensed clinician through AuntEDNA.ai, the same day in most cases.

Method and privacy

Sample package: the school, numbers and quotes on this page are illustrative and show the format of a PulseCheck pilot report. Real reports use the measures agreed with each school before the pilot begins.

All figures are team-level aggregates. Individual athlete data stays with the athlete, their care team and staff they have consented to share with. Screening and clinical details are never shown in department reports. Mental skills scores come from a short athlete self-check at baseline and week 12.

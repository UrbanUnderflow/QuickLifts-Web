04 · Supporting records

Evidence the department can review

Bring the scorecard together with the records behind it. Mental performance records show participation and athlete feedback. Mental care records document screening, concern review and care connections. Authorized reviewers access each record according to their role and the applicable consent rules.

Baseline and screening

Report Starting Point completion here. Record any annual mental-health screening separately, including the instrument, completion date and responsible clinical provider.

Participation

Keep eligible athlete-weeks, completed practices and scheduled Coach Check-Ins alongside the reported percentages.

Support pathway

Keep incident review, care-needed decisions and confirmed connection records distinct. Follow up on pending cases.

Feedback coverage

Show the question, reporting window, response count and eligible population for athlete and coach feedback.

Method and privacy

Sample package: the school, numbers and quotes on this page are illustrative and show the format of an Athletic Mind pilot report. Real reports use the measures, collection methods and targets agreed with each school before the pilot begins. Illustrative values do not establish benchmarks or confirm that every collection workflow is active.

Department reports use approved aggregate data. Individual screening and clinical details follow the applicable consent and access rules. Team Wellness Trend is shown only when the agreed minimum contributor count is met; otherwise it is suppressed. Small incident and feedback groups also require a disclosure review before sharing.

Weekly participation uses eligible athlete-weeks. Weeks with fewer than three eligible days or fewer than three assigned practice opportunities are shown separately from the corresponding three-per-week measure. Count each assigned practice once. Explain pauses, withdrawals and other exclusions alongside the denominator. Overall participation overlaps with the check-in and practice measures and is not a third independent outcome.

For feedback, report the latest response per person in the stated window and show nonresponse. Yes shares use all valid responses to the question. Continuation measures stated intent. Missing observations are shown as unavailable, rather than zero. Baseline completion describes the Starting Point assessment and makes no annual screening compliance claim.
